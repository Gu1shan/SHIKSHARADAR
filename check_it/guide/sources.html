What each source established

NIPUN Bharat (MoE, 2021)

Defines the target: foundational literacy & numeracy by Grade 3 by 2026–27, with "meaningful assessments" and "consistent tracking of learning levels." This is the policy hook for Shiksha Radar.

UDISE+

Largest school census (14.67 lakh schools, 24.72 cr students) — shows scale and that no concept-level data exists at that level.

Saral

Proves answer-sheet digitization at scale (1B+ records) — but capture is marks-only, confirming the analytics gap.

VSK (Gujarat)

Government's own good-practice dashboard — strengths in governance reporting, absent in teacher-facing diagnostics.

ASER 2024

Independent ground-truth on learning outcomes — the problem's severity is externally measured, not self-reported.

DPDP Act 2023

Legal baseline for children's data: consent, purpose limitation, minimisation, storage limitation, safeguards, deletion rights.

Open & honest unknowns

  • Current SIH problem statements & scoring weights: recheck sih.gov.in and siceval.mic.gov.in at submission time; specifics may change between planning and the event.
  • DPDP school-specific consent rules: implementation details continue to be clarified by regulators; the mapping here reflects the Act as published.
  • Offline/LLM behavior under real field connectivity: design decisions are sound but untested until a pilot — stated as such.
