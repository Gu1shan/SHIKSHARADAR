Part 01 · The Problem

India's learning crisis is real and quantified

Before building any solution, we verified the problem from official sources. The evidence is unambiguous — and it points to a specific, solvable gap.

Verified fact: NIPUN Bharat (MoE, 2021) mandates "meaningful assessments" and "consistent tracking of learning levels" to achieve foundational literacy & numeracy (FLN) by Grade 3 by 2026–27 — yet current systems capture enrollment, infrastructure, and aggregate scores, not concept-level error patterns.

Why marks alone are insufficient

Two students can score the same 60% with completely different needs:

Student A: 60% overall        Student B: 60% overall
  Fractions → 8 errors            Fractions → 1 error
    (denominator 5)                Algebra   → 5 errors (sign 3)
  Algebra   → 1 error              Geometry  → 4 errors
  Geometry  → 0 errors             (area/perimeter confusion)

What the teacher needs

"Student A needs fraction-denominator remediation. Student B needs algebra sign rules and geometry clarification."

What dashboards show

"Both 60%. Both need help." — the missing piece is concept-level information.
